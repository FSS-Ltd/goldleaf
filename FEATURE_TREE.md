## Known gaps
- PORTAL-IDENTITY threat boundary: a valid Clerk session does not grant portal access. Future reads and mutations must recheck active application membership; invitations must match an authenticated verified email. Hidden sign-up controls do not restrict Clerk account creation. Selected production sign-in events were observed in the existing Clerk dashboard; event coverage, team access, retention and bulk export remain unverified. See `docs/portal-completion/provider-audit.md`; separate-user cross-family/revocation verification remains required before production use. Role-fenced invalidation is mounted; a development own-membership notice and an isolated-QA staff assignment revocation refreshed open views.
