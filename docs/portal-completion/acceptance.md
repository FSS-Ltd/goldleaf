## Scoped admin browser evidence — 15 November 2026

On production main `812022e867be7f17a0999bb4154999d6fe85676f`, the existing admin Google sign-in succeeded. At 320px and 390px, Today and Account had no horizontal overflow. Mobile More opened by keyboard; Tab reached Invoices, and Escape closed it and returned focus to More. The checked pages had one main landmark and `noindex,nofollow`. Reduced-motion emulation was effective; animation behavior was not verified. The observed idle network included two Clerk `/v1/client` POSTs and no portal-content requests; database query counts were not measured. No accounts or profiles were changed and no test mail was requested; the provider automatically sent a new-device security notification, as recorded in `provider-audit.md`.

Axe-core 4.14 reported no violations on the 390px Today view. At 1280px it reported a serious label-content-name mismatch on the desktop brand link: its `aria-label` omitted the visible role label. The shared `PortalShell` now relies on its image alternative text and visible role text for the link name; candidate authenticated browser retest remains pending. Full screen-reader, zoom, performance, parent and staff acceptance remain open. Clerk CLI was unavailable; subsequent read-only dashboard findings and limits are in `provider-audit.md`.

## Parent onboarding pack gap

The existing invitation and membership onboarding (`PORTAL-ONBOARDING`) is not a complete parent onboarding pack. The original portal plan's onboarding scope covers invitations, verified identity and family access; it does not establish delivery of a full pack. The requested pack, parent self-registration of children, onboarding contacts and policy acknowledgement/review are missing. These are planned feature IDs in [FEATURE_TREE.md](../../FEATURE_TREE.md#parent-onboarding-pack-gaps), not an approved form or policy design.

The current parent home (`app/(portal)/(provider)/parent/page.tsx`) reads existing family data and shows an empty state when no children exist. Child creation is admin-only: `app/(portal)/(provider)/admin/children/page.tsx`, its `actions.ts`, `lib/portal/children.ts` and `portal.create_child` in `db/migrations/0012_children_sessions.sql`. `childInput` in `lib/portal/learning-schemas.ts` takes an ID, family ID and name; the child table contains identity, family, name and creation metadata. No parent registration route or fields for date of birth/age, medical or allergy information, emergency contacts, guardian relationships or authorised collection were found. Account profile identity and invoice billing address are not onboarding contact records.

No portal policy version, acknowledgement/consent record, signer and time, withdrawal/evidence, admin compliance review queue or readiness gate was found. Membership, invitation and invoice histories are not a consent compliance audit. `components/SafeHands.tsx` contains static operational copy about allergies, approved foods and collection; it does not establish portal records. DBS, insurance and safeguarding-policy flags in `content/site.ts` are false. The portal notice and data policy remain unapproved drafts. Required data, policies, permissions, retention and review workflow need client decisions before design or implementation; this inventory makes no legal determination.


## Local authenticated QA preparation — 16 November 2026

The disposable `goldleaf_auth_qa` database passed Neon HTTP single-query and batch transaction probes and a WebSocket committed-notification probe. Local restricted roles had no portal table or sequence grants and no elevated roles. Coverage was checked for 81 application function names; this does not establish coverage for every function signature. The synthetic fixture has parent, staff and admin memberships, two families, staff assigned to one family only, and no children. An earlier separate Clerk development account was created with explicit user approval; at that preparation stage its email remained unverified and no verification mail or sign-in had been completed. Source identity and authorization checks remain in place.

The app responded on `http://localhost:3000` after the process-local hostname and DNS-order fix described in [the rehearsal](rehearsal.md#local-authenticated-portal-transport). Validated local proxy mode now uses a 30-second HTTP deadline; the normal deadline remains five seconds. Nine private local operations passed with a fresh HTTP request at 10.1 seconds, warmed HTTP requests at 0.4–1.0 seconds, and a WebSocket operation at 13.9 seconds. These observations are not production performance measurements. Two attempts to attach the in-app browser timed out or reset its kernel. Native Codex app inspection was blocked by safety controls and was not bypassed. The official browser-open request was queued because the chat was not foregrounded; the user was asked to bring it forward.

Later on 16 November, the in-app browser completed a genuine Google sign-in to the separate development application. The account page showed the Google-connected account as verified, and its authenticated subject matched the synthetic membership fixture. No identity or authorization bypass was used. A subsequent `/portal` request still produced a generic error; the local application log showed the active-membership Neon request timing out after the local 30-second budget. Earlier browser attachment failures above describe the preparation stage and were subsequently overcome.

**Authenticated role and workflow verification remains pending.** Sign-in and the preparation checks do not establish role isolation, actions, live updates or full application acceptance. The user approved the parent workflow design on 16 November, with implementation deferred until alerts and QA are complete. Portal policies remain unwritten and unapproved. The parent onboarding pack remains planned; there are no child or consent records in this fixture.
