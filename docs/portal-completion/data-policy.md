## Decisions before notice approval

1. Name the controller, contact, responsible privacy/request owner and escalation route. Confirm purposes and appropriate lawful bases for each processing activity; assess any special-category data and children's information requirements with an appropriate adviser.
2. Set evidenced retention periods or criteria per category, including rejected enquiries, revoked access, issued records, provider records and backups. Assign owners and document exceptions, legal holds and a review date. Existing enquiry-only 12-month wording does not establish portal retention.
3. Confirm actual role access, staff confidentiality/training, offboarding, supplier contracts, subprocessors, locations/transfers, security measures and incident escalation. Verify authentication audit logs instead of assuming they exist.
4. Document identity verification and handling for access, correction, erasure and other applicable requests, including deadlines, third-party data, issued-record restrictions, exports, backups, exceptions and escalation. Test a synthetic request without exposing other families.
5. Draft the portal-specific notice from approved facts and decisions, including recipients, rights and contact details. Obtain client approval and review before publishing. Record version, approval date and release owner.

No retention period, lawful basis, automated purge or approved notice is asserted here. Portal deployment must remain subject to the client's access and data readiness decision.
