## Information used in the portal

We use information supplied by parents, staff and administrators, as well as information generated when the portal is used. Depending on your role, this can include:

- account identity, verified email, membership, invitations and staff assignments;
- family and child names, session dates, locations, attendance and status;
- staff private notes, learning drafts and updates shared with a family;
- family messages and session-change requests and their resolutions;

## Who can access it

Portal access depends on an authenticated account and active membership. Administrators manage portal records. Parents are scoped to their family; staff access is limited to authorised assignments. Staff private notes and unpublished learning drafts are kept separately from updates shared with families. Session-change requests have a narrower audience than family conversations.
